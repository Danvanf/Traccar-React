- `LICENSE`

## Practical Intent

- People may run, use, and modify the software for local/private use.
- People may also deploy modified versions (including backend/server deployments).
- If users interact with a modified version over a network, AGPLv3 requires making the corresponding source code of that modified version available to those users.

## Commercial Use and AGPLv3

AGPLv3 does **not** prohibit commercial use.

What it does require is copyleft compliance, including network-use source availability for modified deployed versions. In other words, commercial operators can use it, but they must comply with AGPLv3 obligations.

## Important Constraint

You cannot add extra field-of-use restrictions (for example, "no commercial backend use") while distributing under pure AGPLv3 terms.

If you want to prohibit or separately license commercial backend use, use a dual-licensing model with legal counsel (for example: AGPLv3 for open use + separate commercial license for proprietary terms).

When redistributing modified versions, preserve notices and include license information as required by AGPLv3.
